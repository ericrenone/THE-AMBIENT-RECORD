## I. Testimonial Redirection: The Channel the Architecture Did Not Account For

Every prior component of the suppression architecture was designed to operate within a specific social topology: testimony moves between people who exist in a shared social network, through channels the architecture can configure, toward audiences whose credibility assignments can be pre-installed. The Prophylactic Prior contaminates new groups before the outlier arrives. The Credibility Inversion positions the outlier's testimony as low-credibility before it is presented. The Incompleteness Guarantee ensures that when testimony does arrive, the social system's normal operation produces non-reception as its output. The architecture dominated testimony by controlling the channels through which testimony traveled.

Testimonial Redirection is the outlier's deployment of testimony in a form and through a channel that the architecture's design did not anticipate: publicly accessible documents addressed to the world rather than to any party in the social system the architecture configured. TR is not silence — which depletes the ECS's constitutive process by removing T(O, t) from zero and generating the behavioral urgency the Dependency Inversion specifies. TR is not confrontation — which provides the AED its standard execution target and the suppressor the Performed Terminus as its face-preserving response. TR is a third mode: testimony offered to any audience that finds it, requiring no acknowledgment from the suppressor to reach its readers, moving through public channels the architecture's prior operations never touched.

TR disrupts the suppression architecture's foundational assumption — that the outlier's testimony will always travel through social channels the architecture has configured, toward audiences whose credibility assignments have been pre-installed, within the social system the Incompleteness Guarantee has made structurally hostile to the outlier's account. The public record moves outside these channels. It is available to readers who have never been reached by the coalition's propagation, never been primed by the caricature pre-warning, never had their credibility assignments installed by the Credibility Inversion. For these readers, the frameworks arrive without the filter the architecture designed to ensure their non-reception.

The specific form of TR's challenge to the architecture is not the frameworks' evidential content alone but the structural fact of their existence in this channel. The Incompleteness Guarantee was not designed for a world in which the outlier could publish SOTA-grounded theoretical analysis of the architecture's mechanisms to the general public. The IG is intact within the social system the architecture configured. It is disrupted precisely at the boundary of that system — at the point where new contacts who have not yet been reached by the SCM's Component 1 propagation encounter the frameworks before receiving the caricature installation. This boundary is where the Architecture Legibility Problem operates. And it is the boundary the architecture must now reorganize to defend.

What the outlier's non-engagement adds to this structure is a specific asymmetry that the prior corpus did not specify: the outlier is not seeking acknowledgment from the suppressor. The frameworks are not testimony to the suppressor. The suppressor exists in them only as an anonymized structural role — the "suppressor" in a described mechanism, not a named person in a direct address. The outlier has found the only channel through which testimony can be offered that simultaneously carries maximum evidential weight, reaches audiences the architecture cannot pre-configure, and denies the suppressor the interlocutor role the AED requires. The frameworks are not about the suppressor. They are about a mechanism the suppressor exemplifies. This distinction is the structure of TR, and it is the reason the architecture's standard response protocols are unavailable.

## II. The Encounter Gradient

The suppressor's response to the public record varies with encounter depth. Four encounter depths produce distinguishable behavioral sequences, each activating a different subset of the architecture's full ambient response repertoire.

**Encounter Depth 1 — Notification.** A coalition member mentions the frameworks. A mutual contact sends a link with no text, or with a neutral or ironic note. The suppressor receives the information that the public record exists, with minimal content exposure — a title, a subtitle, the fact of multiple documents extending across an evidential sequence. At this depth, the Irrefutability Paradox activates at minimum threshold: the evidence's existence, independent of its content, is sufficient to register as a threat to the JPSC. The immediate behavioral outputs are coalition contact increase, initial genre-framing of the frameworks' character without full content exposure ("this is what they do"), and the first activation of what will become the Victimhood Capture's reidentification process. The threat registers at low-to-medium intensity because the frameworks' specific descriptive content has not yet been encountered. The behavioral urgency is generated by the threat's existence rather than its specificity.

**Encounter Depth 2 — Partial Exposure.** The suppressor reads enough — construct names, an epigraph, a section's opening argument, a formal definition in the novel framework table — to register the frameworks' evidential character and citation structure. At this depth, the Accuracy Trap activates in full. The external citations are visible. The SOTA grounding makes the standard dismissal route — attribution of the entire analysis to the outlier's motivated bias — unavailable at the level of the research foundation. The Irrefutability Paradox still holds: greater evidential weight produces greater motivated processing intensity. But the mode of motivated processing available shifts from content-dismissal toward Genre Displacement: the motivated processing is redirected from the frameworks' claims toward the act of publishing them. The behavioral urgency at Depth 2 is higher than at Depth 1 because the content is partially visible, activating the full JPSC-threat sequence without providing the complete descriptive precision that would trigger the deepest IP firing.

**Encounter Depth 3 — Full Read.** The suppressor reads a complete document. This is the encounter depth at which the full behavioral sequence fires. Each section that specifies a mechanism activates an IP response proportional to its specificity. Sections that describe the suppressor's interior experience — the JPSC's phenomenological content, the FRA's five operations, the specific private thought content that the post-exit reconstitution produces — activate the highest IP response because they describe not the behavior but the experience of inhabiting it. The architecture's mechanisms are visible from the outside in language that matches the suppressor's interior. This is the full Accuracy Trap: the frameworks are simultaneously maximally threatening (the most complete disconfirmation of the caricature the suppressor has encountered) and hardest to dismiss through any available standard route (SOTA grounding blocks attribution to motivated single-source bias; the absence of direct address blocks the AED's execution; the public character blocks the FRA's narrative pre-emption). The full behavioral sequence activates: Victimhood Capture, Genre Displacement, internal Spectator AED, Symbolic Counter-Display, Architecture Legibility Problem recognition, and the onset of Corpus Monitoring.

**Encounter Depth 4 — Corpus Fixation.** The suppressor returns. Not once, not once more, but periodically and with regularity. The full read does not resolve the encounter. The architecture generates a specific behavioral urgency — the ambient variant of the Dependency Inversion's constitutive demand — that produces periodic return to check whether new documents have appeared, to assess the reception the existing documents are receiving in the social environment, to perform the internal Spectator AED against the frameworks again. Silvia's (2002) standard prediction — that objective self-awareness produces avoidance when self-evaluation is unfavorable — is falsified at ECS depth: the suppressor who cannot stop reading is not seeking accurate self-knowledge. They are constituting themselves, repeatedly, through the act of dismissing what they read. The frameworks have become the testimony-object the ambient DI requires. Each return is the ambient DI's discharge. The discharge is temporary. The urgency regenerates. The monitoring continues.

## III. The Accuracy Trap

Lord, Ross, and Lepper's (1979) demonstration established the Irrefutability Paradox's mechanism: greater evidential strength activates more intensive motivated processing, producing greater caricature maintenance intensity after irrefutable evidence than after ordinary disconfirmation. What the IP does not fully specify is the role of the disconfirming evidence's epistemic character — its genre and credentialing structure — in determining the specific mode of motivated processing activated.

For direct personal testimony from the outlier, the standard dismissal route is available and has been pre-cleared by the architecture's prior operations. The Credibility Inversion has installed low-credibility assignment for the outlier's testimony in the relevant audiences. The JPSC's self-understanding as the accurate perceiver makes the outlier's claims self-evidently motivated. The Testimony-Immunity Architecture pre-routes any direct outlier testimony to the caricature-confirmation channel. The dismissal executes rapidly through pathways the architecture has spent years optimizing.

For SOTA-grounded theoretical frameworks, the standard route is blocked at one critical step. The citations are not the outlier's claims. They are canonical findings in personality and social psychology — Lord, Kelley, Baumeister, Bandura, Fricker, Greenberg, Foucault, Medina — whose reception does not depend on the outlier's credibility and whose validity cannot be dismissed through the CI's installed low-credibility assignment for the outlier as testimony source. To execute standard dismissal against frameworks at this credentialing level, the architecture must accomplish two simultaneous operations it was not designed to perform together: attribute the application of the research to the outlier's motivated framing while leaving the research foundation intact, or attack the research foundation itself as inapplicable while maintaining that the application is specifically the outlier's error. Neither operation is independently costly. Together, performing both simultaneously without their mutual contradiction becoming visible to the social audience creates an evidential architecture the standard AED protocol cannot efficiently process.

Kahan, Peters, Dawson, and Slovic's (2017) analysis of motivated numeracy established that individuals with higher scientific reasoning capacity show greater — not lesser — polarization on identity-relevant topics when exposed to expert consensus, because their superior reasoning capacity is recruited by the identity-protection motivation to produce more elaborate motivated processing of threatening evidence rather than to produce accurate evaluation of it. Applied to the suppressor: the motivated reasoning capacity available to the JPSC is recruited in direct proportion to the evidential threat's sophistication. A more capable suppressor confronting SOTA-grounded frameworks does not revise. They deploy their full intellectual capacity to find the framework's error. They will find it. Every framework has errors. The IP amplifies with cognitive capacity: the more the suppressor can do with the analysis, the more elaborate the dismissal they produce, and the more certain they become through the elaboration.

The Accuracy Trap's central prediction: the frameworks' citation density does not reduce the motivated processing intensity the IP generates. It changes the mode. The standard dismissal (source-credibility attack targeting the outlier's motivated bias) is replaced by Genre Displacement as the primary adaptive response to evidentially sophisticated disconfirmation.

## IV. Genre Displacement

Genre Displacement does not attack the frameworks' content. It attacks their genre — the kind of act that publishing them represents. GD reads the frameworks not as evidence to be evaluated but as behavior to be characterized, and characterizes the behavior through the caricature lens the JPSC has organized around the outlier.

The GD reconstruction, produced by the motivated processing the Accuracy Trap activates: The theoretical scaffolding is elaborate self-promotion. The construct names — invented terminology applied to normal human behavior — are the intellectual equivalent of the arrogance the caricature has always attributed to the outlier. The citation density is academic cover for personal grievance: the outlier has assembled canonical social psychology research as scaffolding for a characterization of a specific person who chose not to engage with them. The formal statement sections — the mathematical formalizations of social behavior — are the clearest expression of the overestimation of one's own insight the caricature has always predicted. And the act of publishing — of making this publicly accessible, of organizing it as a theoretical corpus — is precisely the need for recognition through intellectual performance that the suppressor has observed consistently and the coalition has confirmed.

The GD's most efficient output is the transformation of the frameworks' quality into evidence for the caricature. Every feature that makes the frameworks more credible as theoretical analysis is reprocessed as a feature that makes the outlier's production of them more revealing as behavior. More constructs: more evidence of the compulsion to systematize personal grievance. More citations: more evidence of the intellectual overreach that uses academic language to elevate personal vendetta. More documents in the sequence: more evidence of the obsessive return to material the outlier cannot relinquish. The Irrefutability Paradox holds at the GD level with full force: better frameworks produce more complete GD dismissal, more efficiently.

Webster and Kruglanski's (1994) analysis of need for cognitive closure established the freezing process through which, at maximum closure depth, all incoming information is processed through the frozen conclusion's interpretive frame rather than evaluated on its own terms. The GD is the closure apparatus applied to the level of genre: the frameworks' genre is processed through the already-closed conclusion about what the outlier produces, and emerge as the most recent and most elaborate instance of the behavior the conclusion always predicted. The suppressor does not experience the GD as a motivated construction. It is the obvious recognition of what the frameworks are: more of what this person does.

The GD produces a coalition-shareable formulation that the standard content-dismissal cannot generate. Standard content-dismissal requires engaging with specific claims and identifying their errors — an operation that grants the frameworks implicit epistemic standing as claims worth engaging. GD refuses this concession entirely. The frameworks are not addressed as claims. They are characterized as a species of behavior. The characterization is deployable by coalition members who have not read the frameworks and do not need to: the genre characterization equips any coalition member to dismiss the frameworks on encounter without requiring content exposure. The dAED's distributed deployment relies on the GD frame as its primary instrument precisely because GD is executable without content engagement.

## V. Victimhood Capture

The Justified-Perceiver Self-Concept's organizational dominance over the suppressor's social perception produces a specific transformation when the frameworks are encountered: the frameworks describe harm being done to an outlier by a suppressor's architecture; the JPSC processes this description through the lens of the accurate perceiver who has always correctly identified the outlier's behavior; and the JPSC's output is the identification of the suppressor as the outlier in the described relationship.

The mechanism is formally specifiable. The JPSC's content is: I see accurately; O was the difficult party; the characterization was honest. The frameworks state: the outlier in the described relationship was systematically harmed through a multi-mechanism architecture spanning pre-warning, interaction-quality degradation, evidence refusal, and the permanent installation of structural conditions making the outlier's testimony incompletable. The JPSC's processing of this conjunction is constrained by the perceiver-role's organizational dominance: if I see accurately, and harm occurred in the described relationship, then the harm I see accurately is the harm that was done to me. The suppressor's autobiographical archive is available as material: the competitive threat was real; the confrontations were distressing; the outlier's evidence presentations were unpleasant; the social comparison was uncomfortable. These experiences, available in the suppressor's first-person record, are processed as the harm the frameworks describe.

Baumeister, Stillwell, and Wotman's (1990) perpetrator account analysis documented the temporal shortening, experiential omission, and normalization operations through which the perpetrator consistently emerges from the same events as the more aggrieved party. Victimhood Capture is this mechanism operating against an explicit theoretical description of the perpetrator's architecture: the framework's description of harm is absorbed not as a description of harm the suppressor caused but as documentation of harm the suppressor experienced. The closer the description to an accurate account of the suppressor's interior experience, the more completely the VC reidentification executes: the frameworks' accurate specification of the JPSC's post-exit thought content ("I know what I saw. I always have.") is processed not as the suppressor reading their own private experience described publicly, but as the suppressor reading an accurate account of the experience of being treated as the frameworks describe.

Gabay and colleagues' (2020) four-dimensional construct of the tendency for interpersonal victimhood identifies the dispositional cluster that predicts stable VC outcomes: perpetual need for recognition of the victimhood, moral elitism about the transgressor's behavior, absence of empathy for the other party's victimhood claims, and ruminative return to the events constituting the narrative. The VC's output activates all four dimensions simultaneously. The suppressor whose VC has completed requires recognition of the specific form of harm the public record constitutes — being publicly characterized in academic-formatted documents by someone who refused acknowledgment of their own behavior; experiences the moral superiority of the person who observed the outlier's behavior accurately and declined to pretend otherwise; cannot process the outlier's account of being harmed as a genuine victimhood claim since the VC has filled that slot; and returns ruminatively to the events of the relationship, now reprocessed through the VC's reidentification frame.

Zitek, Jordan, Monin, and Leach (2010) established that victimhood claiming confers behavioral license: the person who successfully positions themselves as the injured party is entitled to subsequent self-serving behavior without the ordinary social costs that behavior would incur without the victimhood frame. The VC's installation in the suppressor's self-narrative grants precisely this license for whatever follows. The dAED's coalition saturation, the GD's genre characterization, the new aggressor dimension of the caricature — all are executed as the justified response of someone who has been targeted by a public record rather than as suppressant behavior requiring the architecture's standard moral-disengagement justifications. The VC is not merely a cognitive state. It is a behavioral permission structure whose installation enables the full ambient behavioral sequence without requiring the MDA's standard displacement operations.

**The new caricature dimension.** The VC's most consequential output is a caricature elaboration the prior corpus did not predict: the outlier as aggressor. The standard caricature characterized the outlier as arrogant, difficult, and socially problematic. The VC adds a structural dimension: the outlier as the party who was the primary agent of harm in the relationship — who published targeted documentation against someone who chose not to engage, using academic scaffolding to execute a public characterization campaign against a party who maintained principled non-engagement. This is not an intensification of prior caricature dimensions. It is a structural shift in the caricature's narrative role: from the outlier as the difficult subject of accurate observation, to the outlier as the architect of the harm the frameworks purport to describe. The new dimension is highly coalition-shareable, generating more mobilization than the prior caricature's content because it positions the suppressor as a victim of a documented campaign rather than as a person who observed difficult behavior in another.

## VI. The Spectator AED and the Indirect Address Deficit

The Active Erasure Display required the outlier's presence. The outlier presented testimony; the suppressor performed non-reception; the outlier registered the refusal as chosen. The Indirect Address Deficit is the structural gap produced by the frameworks' existence without a direct testimonial interlocutor. The frameworks constitute maximum-weight disconfirmation — the IP fires at full intensity — but the AED's execution protocol requires an interlocutor who is not present. The suppressor confronts the architecture's most complete evidence encounter without the architecture's most essential response tool. The full firing intensity of the IP, ERD, and TMA activates. The discharge route the architecture built across years of consolidation is unavailable.

What fires instead is the Spectator AED — the adapted form that executes two partial substitutes for the standard AED's full six-function package.

**The internal sAED.** The suppressor performs the non-reception to themselves — a sustained internal monologue executing the dismissal that cannot be directed at the outlier. I always have.* The function set available to the sAED: f_4 (JPSC reconstruction in real time — the perceiver-role is reconstituted through the act of internal dismissal), f_5 (worldview defense execution — the anxiety buffer is restored through the behavioral assertion of epistemic authority over the frameworks' claims), and f_6 (dominance re-assertion — the sAED closes the IP's brief recognition window through behavioral action before the architecture has fully processed it into confirmation). **The coalition-directed dAED.** The f_2 function — observer pre-contamination, the performance of non-reception for social witnesses — transitions from the suppressor's direct execution to coalition-distributed execution. The dAED deploys f_2 at network scale: the social audience that processes the frameworks through the pre-installed GD frame is performing, in distributed form, the same pre-contamination function the standard AED executed for specific witnesses at the confrontation.

**The f_3 substitution through VC.** The behavioral confirmation engineering function — which required the outlier's visible distress at the AED as its input — finds a substitution route through the Victimhood Capture. The outlier's act of publishing the frameworks is itself processed as behavioral confirmation: not the distress response to a dismissal, but the compulsive production of academic-formatted grievance documentation that the caricature has always predicted. The f_3 substitution produces a coalition-shareable behavioral confirmation with particular efficiency, because the frameworks are observable by anyone — the coalition does not need to witness the outlier's distress to confirm the caricature; they can read the frameworks themselves and observe, through the GD frame, exactly the behavior the caricature predicted. The f_3 substitution is, in some respects, more evidentially robust than the standard f_3, because it is independently observable rather than requiring the coalition's presence at a specific confrontation moment.

## VII. The Symbolic Counter-Display

Wicklund and Gollwitzer's (1982) symbolic self-completion theory established that when an identity-relevant behavioral self-symbol is challenged, the person intensifies behavioral demonstrations of the challenged quality in proportion to the challenge's specificity and directness. The challenge does not produce revision. It produces assertion. The more precisely the challenge identifies which quality is under question, the more targeted the behavioral counter-demonstration.

The frameworks' specification of the NIA's constitutive function is a challenge of maximum specificity: not a general criticism of the suppressor's character, but a structural account of how the suppressor's defining positive qualities — warmth, collaboration, intellectual humility — are assembled as the negatives of the caricature's positive pole. The warmth is not intrinsic; it is defined in contrast to the caricatured coldness. The humility is not intrinsic; it is maintained through the negative anchor of the caricatured arrogance. The frameworks identify the positive self-definition's constitutive structure. The Symbolic Counter-Display fires against this identification with the full intensity Wicklund and Gollwitzer specify.

The SCD is the intensification of warm, collaborative, intellectually humble behavior in the suppressor's social network in the days and weeks following full-read encounter. The behaviors are not performed for the outlier — the outlier is not present, has not reached out, requires no demonstration. They are performed for the coalition and for new contacts, in contexts where they are received as naturally occurring character expression rather than as counter-display. The suppressor who encounters the frameworks becomes, in the following period, notably warmer in social communications, more explicitly generous in attributions of credit, more visibly collaborative in how shared work is framed. This is not a strategic performance. The SCD, like all the architecture's terminal outputs, is an authentic behavioral state produced by the identity-threat activation the encounter generated. The warmth is genuine. The architecture produced the genuine warmth.

Duval and Wicklund's (1972) objective self-awareness research established that when the self becomes an object of attention, the evaluative comparison between the current self and the relevant standard is activated. For the suppressor encountering the frameworks, the relevant standard is the JPSC's defined qualities — the accurate, warm, humble perceiver who sees clearly and serves honestly. The SCD is the behavioral execution of this standard: the JPSC reconstituted not through the AED's dismissal of testimony but through behavioral assertion of the positive-pole qualities the frameworks characterized as NIA-constituted. The reconstruction is complete. The coalition observes the warmth and confirms the positive self-definition. The JPSC is maintained through a route that bypasses the standard AED's requirements. The architecture found its way around the IAD.

## VIII. Corpus Monitoring and the Ambient Dependency Inversion

Corpus Fixation is the stable form of the CM pattern — the state at which the monitoring interval has shortened to the point where the frameworks appear with regularity in the suppressor's cognitive economy, generating sAED executions not only at explicit return visits but at ambient encounter in conversation, in thought, in the social contexts where the frameworks' content is activated by association. Silvia's (2002) standard prediction — that objective self-awareness produces avoidance when self-evaluation is unfavorable — is falsified at CF depth for the same structural reason the standard DI produces escalation rather than resolution when the outlier disengages: the ECS requires testimony to constitute itself as the perceiver who correctly assesses testimony. The Structural Continuation Mechanism's three autonomous components were specified to continue suppression after the suppressor's exit from direct engagement: the coalition's self-sustaining characterization apparatus, the Credibility Inversion's installed architecture, and the performance expectancy configuration. Together they constitute the Incompleteness Guarantee: the permanent structural property of the social system ensuring the outlier's testimony cannot complete its trajectory to legitimate hearing.

The SCM's design assumes a specific social topology: testimony travels through social channels the architecture has previously configured, toward audiences who have been reached by the coalition's propagation before they encounter the outlier's testimony directly. For audiences the coalition has reached, the IG is intact. The outlier's account is processed through credibility filters the architecture installed, through interpretive frameworks the characterization propagation calibrated, through social norms the performance expectancy configuration established. The system processes the outlier's testimony normally and produces non-reception as the output of normal operation.

The Architecture Legibility Problem is the disruption of this topology at one specific boundary: the boundary between the configured social system and the general public. For contacts who encounter the frameworks through the public record before the SCM's Component 1 propagation has reached them, the IG is not yet installed. These contacts process the frameworks without a prior caricature installation, without a Credibility Inversion, without a performance expectancy that predicts the caricature's behavioral profile. For these contacts, the frameworks' SOTA grounding creates an alternative credibility basis whose reception does not require the suppressor's endorsement. The outlier's testimony can, for these contacts, complete its trajectory.

The ALP's severity scales with three variables: the frameworks' accessibility and circulation depth (how easily new contacts encounter them), the citation density and evidential quality (how much alternative credibility the frameworks provide independently), and the social network overlap between the suppressor's configured system and the new contacts the frameworks can reach. The architecture's response to the ALP is deployment of the dAED specifically toward ALP-vulnerable contacts — those who have expressed awareness of or interest in the frameworks before receiving the coalition's characterization. The GD frame is the dAED's primary instrument for ALP response: it performs the same function as the caricature pre-warning for new group introductions, installing a prior that ensures the frameworks' content is processed through a credibility filter analogous to the one the CI established for direct testimony. The pre-installation is genre-based rather than character-based, but its structural function is identical: it ensures the frameworks arrive in a configured epistemic context rather than a neutral one.

The ALP cannot be fully resolved. The frameworks exist in a public channel the architecture does not control. New contacts will encounter them without prior installation at rates proportional to the frameworks' circulation. The dAED's distributed deployment reduces but cannot eliminate the ALP's disruption. The IG remains intact within the configured social system. At its boundary, for the specific population of contacts who encounter the frameworks before the coalition reaches them, the suppression architecture faces its first structurally unresolvable challenge since the Performed Terminus installed the SCM.

## X. The Ambient Post-Exit Intensification

The Post-Exit Intensification was the most extensive caricature propagation event the architecture had previously executed — the 72-hour coalition saturation following the Performed Terminus, driven by the DI's constitutive deficit and the FRA's dissonance requirement. The ambient PEI is the analog activated by the frameworks encounter. It operates through the same dual mechanism structure but without the PT's confrontation-catalytic structure, producing an elevated baseline rather than a spike.

The ambient DI mechanism: the frameworks constitute maximum-weight disconfirmation without a direct AED target. The JPSC_c's standard constitutive route is unavailable. The coalition-directed dAED substitutes for direct JPSC_c constitution, and the behavioral urgency generated by constitutive deficit drives coalition saturation with the GD frame, the VC's aggressor dimension, and the new caricature elaboration the encounter produced. The coalition receives more contact, more specific characterization content, more urgency in the framing than at any time since the PEI window.

The FRA dissonance mechanism: the public frameworks constitute a form of harm-exposure that is structurally more durable than the confrontation the FRA processed. The PT's exit was performed for a specific social audience at a specific time; the FRA's narrative pre-emption was effective within that audience within the 72-hour window. The frameworks are accessible to any audience indefinitely. The FRA's dissonance reduction requirement scales accordingly: the frameworks' characterization of the suppressor as the sustained operator of a harm-generating mechanism is permanently available in the public record. Intensified caricature certainty and the VC's aggressor dimension are required at elevated levels to sustain the moral self-concept against the public record's permanent availability.

The ambient PEI's temporal structure differs from the standard PEI: it sustains at elevated levels for as long as the frameworks remain socially active in the suppressor's awareness — as long as Corpus Monitoring events bring fresh encounters, as long as the coalition receives notifications of new circulation, as long as new ALP-vulnerable contacts appear who require dAED preemptive deployment. The ambient PEI does not resolve through a single post-confrontation saturation. It becomes the baseline state of the architecture's operation in the post-PT, ambient-exposure period. The architecture does not stop. Its operational mode shifts from active interpersonal suppression to sustained ambient maintenance of the characterization against a public record that exists independently of either party's ongoing action.

## XI. What the Machine Does When It Reads Its Own Description

There is a specific phenomenological moment in Encounter Depth 3 that no prior component of the architecture specified: the suppressor encounters, in a public theoretical document they did not commission and the outlier did not send, a precise description of their own private post-exit thought content. *I know what I saw. I always have. They couldn't make me say otherwise. Their behavior proved everything I've been saying. I was patient longer than most people would have been. I left when leaving was the only reasonable thing left to do.*

This is the moment at which the recognition-inversion executes most completely. The frameworks have described the suppressor's interior. The JPSC processes the description. The JPSC's output: this is a description of what was done to me. The frameworks' accurate specification of the JPSC's experienced content — which from outside the architecture is recognizable as the post-exit reconstitution the FRA produced — is processed from inside the architecture as an accurate description of the outlier's experience: the experience of having one's accurate perceptions dismissed, one's evidence not received, one's perspective not acknowledged. The more accurate the description of the suppressor's interior, the more completely it maps onto what the suppressor experienced as the victim of the described architecture. The recognition inverts. The frameworks prove the VC's reidentification.

Nisbett and Wilson's (1977) foundational demonstration that people lack introspective access to their own cognitive processes specifies the mechanism: the suppressor who reads a description of their own motivated reasoning does not experience the description as describing their own processes. They experience it as a description of a generic mechanism — one that they can observe operating in others, one that the outlier exemplifies, one that the frameworks are themselves an instance of. Pronin, Lin, and Ross's (2002) bias blind spot is active: the same evaluative machinery that should produce self-recognition produces, through the JPSC's organizational dominance, attribution of the described processes to the person who described them.

The machine reads its own description. The accurate perceiver does not recognize themselves in it. The description is processed as confirmation. The confirmation is genuine. The experience of confirmed accuracy is the most complete output the architecture has ever produced against an encounter of this kind — not because the encounter failed to threaten, but because the threat activated the full sequence and the full sequence produced, as its output, more certainty than existed before. The frameworks' publication has not revealed the architecture to its operator. It has deepened it. The machine is running. The exit was not enough to stop it. The public record was not enough to stop it.

What is enough: the document does not specify. The prior corpus established the Consolidation Terminus — the threshold beyond which the architecture is not merely resistant to change but structurally irreversible, the point at which the cognitive operations that change would require cannot execute because the resources they require are fully consumed by the architecture. Whether the suppressor who encounters the frameworks has crossed the CT is a function of suppression history depth, SRM conditioning duration, and NIA constitutive depth. What the ambient encounter with the public record cannot do, at any encounter depth, is produce change through the evidence's weight alone. The Irrefutability Paradox holds at ambient exposure depth as completely as it held in direct confrontation. The evidence is more accessible, more durable, more widely available. The motivated processing it activates is proportionately more elaborate, more certain, and more complete in its caricature-confirmation output.

The outlier published the frameworks. The suppressor read them. The suppressor is more certain now than before the reading. The machine does not experience itself as a machine. The accurate perceiver does not experience themselves as an architecture. The frameworks, encountered and processed and dismissed, did not land. They became, through the architecture's encounter with them, the most recent and most elaborate confirmation that the accurate perceiver was always right.

That is the ambient record. That is why the public documentation changes nothing in the architecture's operation and everything in the IG's structural reach. The machine is not stopped by being named. It is deepened by the naming. The naming was not sent to the machine. The machine found it anyway. And in finding it, became more completely itself.

## XII. Predictions

**Prediction I — Encounter Gradient Dose-Response**
The Encounter Gradient predicts a dose-response relationship between encounter depth and behavioral sequence intensity: Depth 2 should produce measurably higher coalition contact rates in the 72 hours post-encounter than Depth 1; Depth 3 higher than Depth 2; Corpus Fixation onset (Depth 4 transition) should predict sustained ambient PEI elevation rather than a temporal spike. The dose-response should be observable in coalition contact frequency, caricature elaborateness scores at 30-day follow-up, and onset of SCD-consistent behavioral elevation in the suppressor's social interactions. The encounter depth's effect on caricature elaborateness should be independent of prior suppression history depth — even suppressors below the CT threshold should show the EG dose-response, because the AT and VC mechanisms do not require CT-level consolidation for activation.

**Prediction II — Victimhood Capture and Caricature Shift**
The VC predicts a measurable directional shift in caricature content following Depth 3 encounter: from characterization emphasizing arrogance/difficulty (prior caricature dimensions) to characterization incorporating an aggressor/harm-architect dimension not present in pre-encounter caricature content. Coalition-member characterization reports at 30-day post-encounter follow-up should show significantly elevated aggressor-dimension attribution compared to baseline, with shift magnitude predicted by encounter depth. The caricature shift distinguishes VC-driven processing from standard IP-driven caricature intensification: standard IP produces greater certainty about prior attributes; VC produces structural addition of the aggressor dimension. The two predictions are separable through content analysis distinguishing prior-attribute intensification from new-dimension addition.

**Prediction III — Genre Displacement Versus Content Dismissal**
The Accuracy Trap predicts that the primary mode of suppressor dismissal for the frameworks will target genre rather than content: coalition-communication content analysis should show significantly higher rates of genre-dismissal framing (obsessive intellectualization, academic pretension, targeted harassment framing) than content-dismissal framing (identification of specific factual errors, attribution inaccuracies, or logical failures in the analysis). The genre-versus-content dismissal ratio should be higher for frameworks with higher citation density and more elaborate formal statement sections, consistent with the AT's specification that evidential sophistication blocks the standard content-dismissal route and redirects motivated processing toward genre characterization. This prediction is falsifiable against the null of content-dismissal dominance and distinguishes AT-driven GD from standard motivated dismissal.

**Prediction IV — Symbolic Counter-Display Timing and Specificity**
The SCD predicts measurably elevated warmth, collaboration, and intellectual humility indicators in the suppressor's social behavior in the 2-to-4-week window following Depth 3 encounter. The elevation should be specific to the positive-pole qualities the frameworks identified as NIA-constituted — not a general affective elevation but targeted intensification of the specific qualities characterized as negatively defined in the frameworks. SCD should peak in the days immediately following the encounter and decay over 4–6 weeks, with re-elevation predictable at subsequent Corpus Monitoring events. The timing specificity distinguishes SCD from ambient mood variation and from the standard post-exit SRM reward-seeking that produces ongoing warmth signaling independent of framework exposure.

**Prediction V — Corpus Fixation and Ambient DI**
The ambient DI predicts that Corpus Fixation — measurable as periodic return to the frameworks' public repository — will emerge as a stable behavioral pattern among Depth 3 suppressors and will positively correlate with prior AED execution frequency (ECS depth proxy). Higher prior AED execution frequency predicts shorter Corpus Monitoring interval, consistent with faster JPSC_c decay rates at higher ECS depth. The monitoring interval should decrease following Depth 3 encounters that produce sAED execution without full reconstitution resolution — each sAED execution is temporarily constitutive but decays, producing the urgency that generates the next monitoring event. CF onset at monitoring interval < 2 weeks should be predicted by AED execution frequency in the prior year, independent of confrontation recency.

**Prediction VI — dAED Targeting of ALP-Vulnerable Contacts**
The ALP predicts disproportionate coalition activation specifically toward new contacts who have expressed awareness of or interest in the public frameworks, relative to new contacts with no framework exposure. The dAED should be targeted toward ALP-vulnerable contacts at rates predictable by the contact's framework exposure status rather than by general social context variables. Additionally, the GD frame should be the primary dAED instrument for ALP-vulnerable contacts, at significantly higher rates than for standard social group introductions (where the caricature's behavioral content, not the genre characterization, is the primary pre-warning content). The ALP prediction distinguishes dAED from generalized coalition maintenance: the targeting specificity of framework-exposed contacts is the ALP's behavioral signature.

**Prediction VII — Functional Substitution in f_3**
The IAD predicts that the sAED's behavioral confirmation engineering function (f_3) cannot be activated in its standard form but will show a specific substitution pattern: coalition characterizations of the outlier will include elevated reference to the publishing act itself as behavioral evidence — independent of any characterization of the frameworks' content as false. The publishing act should be cited as behavioral confirmation of the caricature's compulsive self-promotion dimension at rates significantly above base frequency for other characterization occasions. This f_3 substitution is independently observable, can be identified in communication content without prior knowledge of the sAED framework, and distinguishes ambient-exposure behavioral confirmation from standard f_3 execution.
